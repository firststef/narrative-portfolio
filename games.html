Games

The games I have made.

Streamer UNTIL

Unity · March 2026 · game jam

A horror game about a streamer doing urban exploration in an abandoned building. On a four-person team I wrote the chat barks and built the node-based dialogue system that hands the player dares based on the environment. The simulated audience is the antagonist: its viewership meter keeps you alive, and the higher it climbs, the harder the monster hunts you.

Chess Worlds

Godot · February 2026 to present

A tactics game where chess pieces come to life and fight across a living world. You play a lone human king, the last survivor after his kingdom falls in a single night, and travel across four realms gathering allies against the demons and their blight. Each of the five factions plays by its own philosophy: elves foresee, dwarves endure, mermaids reshape the board, constructs run on programmable logic.

ChessWorlds: Grandmaker

Godot · July 2026 · prototype

A chess variant where you balance two armies and design your own pieces out of code blocks, then send them into battle. It grew out of Chess Worlds and turns piece design itself into the game.

Realm Code

PlayCanvas · January 2026 · built in a seven-day jam

A short game where magic is written as code. A parasite living in the code has corrupted your garden, and you heal it by finding books, completing the code, and writing your own inside it. Made from scratch for a jam on the theme "glitch in the system," which is also where I learned PlayCanvas.

Spel Ignite

Unity · 2021 · prototype

A visual-programming game built on SPEL, an esoteric language I made in college. You write real logic to cast spells in a small world I wanted to fill with mystery and characters. It is a prototype, and the first piece of a larger idea.

The Untitled Story: the video game

RPG Maker · 2018 · about six months

A fan game I built on my own from Kevin Do's concept art. I pieced together the story, wrote all the dialogue and the scenes, and mixed role-playing with fighting-game combat for stickman characters. Now discontinued, but the writing was all mine.
